## Install in 3 steps

1. **Download** the file with the button above and double-click it. You do not need to be an administrator.
2. Windows may say **"Windows protected your PC"**. This is normal for a beta, because the app is not signed yet.
   Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Click **Next** and **Install**, then **Finish**. OASIS opens, and its first-run guide takes you through your pad,
   your microphone and a first dictation.

The first time OASIS starts it downloads its voice models (a few hundred MB, up to about 1.2 GB, once). Keep
an internet connection on until the guide says it is ready.

**What you need:** Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), a microphone, a PC with at least 4 processor threads and 4 GB of
memory (6 GB or more for the most accurate voice), and about 3 GB of free disk space.

## Updates

OASIS checks for a new version when it starts and every 12 hours. When there is one, it asks **Update now** or
**Later**. Nothing installs until you press **Update now**.

## Moving from an older version?

Quit OASIS from the tray (right-click its icon, **Quit OASIS**), save your profiles, remove the old version, install
this one and copy your profiles back. [The guide](UNINSTALL-AND-BACKUP.md) walks you through it.
